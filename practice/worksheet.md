# Switching-tank worksheet

All data and the controller model are fictional. A tank starts at 10 L. Its rate in L/min is $6-t^2$ on $[0,2)$ and $2(t-4)$ on $[2,7]$. Prepare your first trace without AI or the answer key.

1. Sketch rate, labeling open and filled switch endpoints and units. Write the two limits and the value at minute 2. Predict where volume rises or falls.
2. Derive the first branch's flow derivative with a secant quotient. Explain why that derivative is not the slope of the volume graph.
3. Compute the piecewise volume and verify both continuity and the two one-sided slopes at minute 2. Build a candidate table and name the absolute extrema.
4. Compute midpoint estimates with one-minute and half-minute panels. Report each signed error against the exact change. Explain why the unsplit one-minute trapezoid returns 11 L and why the isolated sample cannot change the exact answer.
5. A draft memo claims minute 2 is the highest level and the final volume is 11 L. Reject or revise each sentence using independent checks: an endpoint comparison and a split area calculation.

After recording the derivation, you may use a peer, calculator or AI assistant to challenge one line. Grade the revised evidence, not whether you agree with the tool. Cite the original model, show your calculation and disclose the assistance.

## Release after the first attempt

A replacement controller uses the same first branch but supplies $2(t-3)$ from minute 2 onward. The observation ends at minute 4. Recompute the candidate values and report **every** time with an absolute maximum. Verify your conclusion with signed area on $[2,4]$. Do not reuse the old right-hand slope or forget the changed endpoint. [Key](answer-key.md)
